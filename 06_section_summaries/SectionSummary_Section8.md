## Argument Structure

### Opening
Picks up Section 7's procedural norms. Announces three limitations requiring acknowledgment: scope, implementation, evidential base.

### Limitation 1: Scope (Internal Norms Only)
- Liberal constitution as developed addresses *how* AI reasons, not *embedding* in collective practices
- Mercier's framework implies both dimensions
- System could implement all Section 7 principles yet lack: verification, debate, empirical testing
- Safeguards (observation, experimentation, logical scrutiny, debate) require infrastructure beyond norms

**Key framing:** Limitation of scope, not gap in argument. Paper develops internal because finding is internal (suppression/inversion = failures in reasoning about sources). External = complementary future work. Liberal framework privileges neither.

### Limitation 2: Implementation Agnosticism
- Principles neutral on mechanism (training, prompting, constitutional constraint, architecture)
- Agnosticism deliberate: paper identifies what was observed, why it matters, what norms should govern
- *How* to achieve norms = separate question, different methods

**Honest acknowledgment:** Leaves open whether norms achievable through current approaches. Asymmetries might reflect training distributions constitutional constraints cannot override. Empirical questions about implementation ≠ normative questions about reasonable policies.

### Limitation 3: Evidential Base
- 21 evaluations, two model families, six topics
- Findings replicated (>90% detection in clean protocols)
- But: limited sample, topic clustering (policy domains), specific source conditions

**Spoiled evaluations:** Strengthen rather than weaken—reveal suppression mechanism. But suggest behavioral findings may be unstable (systems may behave differently in deployment than evaluation).

### Closing
Limitations bound contribution without undermining it. Paper argues one is needed + develops source-attending component. Framework, derivation, principles stand on arguments, not comprehensiveness of evidential base.
