### Scanlon (1998)

**Full reference:** Scanlon, T. M. (1998). *What We Owe to Each Other*. Harvard University Press.

**Usage in section:** Source of contractualist test adapted for epistemic norms. Consequences matter as reasons individuals can press against principles, not as aggregate score. Reasonable rejectability as test for normative legitimacy.

**Function:** Provides the normative test structure that defines the Liberal approach.

**Relation to Complete Prompt:** Listed in Section IV (Literature Placement). The Scanlonian epistemic principle was developed in ChatGPT conversation (Document 016) and preserved in Complete Prompt Section XI.

**Quality notes:** Primary source; canonical text in contractualist ethics.

**Function:** Shows the Scanlonian move has epistemic precedent; not just imported from ethics.
