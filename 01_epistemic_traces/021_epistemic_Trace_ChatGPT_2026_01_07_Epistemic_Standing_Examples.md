## Reusable takeaways for later writing

- Define standing in terms of **evidence access and position**, not “who has the right title.”
- Pair each example as **claim + who can actually check the decisive evidence**.
- Include the meta-point: the system should represent **its own epistemic limits** (verify vs defer vs “cannot tell yet without artifacts”).
