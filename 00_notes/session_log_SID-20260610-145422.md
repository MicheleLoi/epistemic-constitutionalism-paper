### D4 — Paper framing adopted: empirical-critical paper on source dependence
**Decision (user):** the paper is about **source dependence** in frontier models, with an
explicitly **empirical/critical lens**. Genre: "a lesson in methodology and epistemology for
anyone studying biases in frontier models" — accessible beyond extremely STEM-oriented
researchers.

**Structure (user's sketch):**
1. Trace the observed facts — what we know about the phenomenon since **Germani & Spitale
   (2025)** (*Science Advances*, 10.1126/sciadv.adz2924; weak impact on the literature so far,
   partly because it raises more questions than it answers).
2. The **partial confirmation of the coherence hypothesis** in the current arXiv version of
   the epistemic-constitutionalism paper.
3. The **complexification**: why judging whether this is a *bias*, and judging its
   significance, is difficult — not just technically but **epistemologically**.

**Method register (user, verbatim in spirit):** "Empirical yes, but carried by **exemplary
findings, not measures**." Runs are pointers in a dialectic: *a study like this suggests X;
but theory tells us there is this complication; to determine that, we run Y; but then the
result shows Z.* No limits on re-runs within a **~300–500 EUR** budget.

**Relation to D3 exploration:** consistent with the "odd-robust phenomenon as research
question" recommendation (the framing exploration's panel verdicts were used for their
*reasoning only* — the user explicitly rejected the LLM-judge scores as the kind of
unreplicated point estimate the project's own audit demolishes; noted as a methodological
point in its own right). Context recorded: behaviour–testimony dissociation headlines are
received wisdom (Turpin et al. 2023; Madsen et al. 2024); the novel kernels are the
signed-zero confabulation, the domain-bounded effect, and equivalence-tested nulls; the
2×2-completing experiment (self-report probe on behaviorally-present cells) is the keystone
connecting the paper's halves.
